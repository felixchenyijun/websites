What we do

Lawn, tree and shrub health. That's the whole business.

Six services, each one a program we plan around your lawn and the season, not a one-size treatment sprayed on every yard on the street.

Fertilization

Nitrogen for color and growth, phosphorus for roots, potash for wear and weather. Fast release for the quick green, slow release for the long haul, and a fall application that sets up next spring.

Weed control

The best defense is a good offense. Pre-emergent for annuals like dandelion and clover, post-emergent for perennial broadleaf weeds, and a thick, fed lawn that wins the fight for light and water on its own.

Crabgrass control

Crabgrass germinates with every watering and crowds out real turf. We put down pre-emergent in season and treat post-emergent as needed. Heavy spring rain or a brutal summer can cut any product's effect, and we'll tell you so.

Insect control

Preventative or curative, applied once a year for grubs, chinch bugs and other surface feeders. Flea and tick treatments break the cycle in the yard, which is where your dog picks them up.

Core aeration

Compacted soil starves roots of oxygen and keeps water and fertilizer on the surface. We pull small cores so air, moisture and nutrients get down to the root zone, in drought and in heavy rain.

Tree & shrub

Trees and shrubs usually live off the fertilizer meant for the lawn, and both lose out. We feed them directly, groundcovers included, and use deep root feeding where a tree needs it.
