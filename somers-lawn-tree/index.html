Good to know

Questions we get a lot

Q.Do you do landscaping, tree work and lawn care?

Yes. Flower beds, shrub and bush removal, brush and ground prep; dead limbs and whole trees; and keeping the lawn cut — all the same crew, so you’re not chasing three companies for one property.

Q.How do I get a price?

Call or text Tom at (740) 463-8025, or use the form below to put together a text. For tree and bed work we’ll usually take a look first, then give you a straight number before any work starts.

Q.What hours can I reach you?

We’re reachable seven days a week, from early morning to late evening. If a storm drops a limb in your yard, you don’t have to wait until Monday to get someone on the phone.

Q.Do you clean up afterward?

Yes. Clippings, branches and brush get cleared out as part of the job — one reviewer put it as “cleaned up immaculately,” and that’s the standard.

Q.Do you sell firewood?

When we have it. A lot of our firewood comes from the trees we take down, so supply varies — ask when you call and we’ll let you know what’s on hand.

Q.What areas do you cover?

Washington Court House and the surrounding Fayette County towns — Jeffersonville, Bloomingburg, Milledgeville, New Holland, Sabina and nearby. Not sure if you’re in range? Just ask.
