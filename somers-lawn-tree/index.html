Good to know

Questions we get a lot

Q.Do you do both lawns and tree work?

Yes — that’s the whole point. The same crew that mows your lawn can take down a dead limb or a whole tree, so you’re not chasing separate companies for one property.

Q.How do I get a price?

Call Tom at (740) 463-8025 or send the quick form on this page. For tree and bed work we’ll usually take a look first, then give you a fair, upfront estimate before any work starts.

Q.What hours can I reach you?

We’re reachable seven days a week, from early morning to late evening. If a storm drops a limb in your yard, you don’t have to wait until Monday to get someone on the phone.

Q.Do you clean up afterward?

Always. Clippings, branches and brush get cleared out as part of the job — we leave the yard looking finished, not like a worksite.

Q.Do you sell firewood?

When we have it. A lot of our firewood comes from the trees we take down, so supply is seasonal — ask when you call and we’ll let you know what’s on hand.

Q.What areas do you cover?

Washington Court House and the surrounding Fayette County towns — Jeffersonville, Bloomingburg, Milledgeville, New Holland, Sabina and nearby. Not sure if you’re in range? Just ask.
