How We Work

The plan comes first. The patio, the wall, the planting bed — that's just us building what we already designed together.

We're a family-owned design-build crew based right here in Annapolis, working hardscape and landscape jobs across Anne Arundel County — Arnold, Crownsville, Edgewater, Severna Park, Odenton, Pasadena, and the neighborhoods around them. Every project starts the same way: we walk your yard, talk through what you actually want to use the space for, and put it into a real design before a single stone gets set.

We handle the whole job with our own crew — site plans, material selection, hardscape construction, and the planting and grading around it — so you're working with one contractor from the first sketch to the last stone, not three subcontractors passing the job around.
