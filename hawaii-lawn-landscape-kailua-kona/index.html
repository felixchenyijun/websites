What we do

From a new front yard to the irrigation that keeps it alive.

Design, installation, irrigation and upkeep — the four things we get asked for most around Kailua-Kona, and the ones our certifications cover.

Landscape design & installation

New beds, plantings and whole front- or back-yard builds. We pick plants that take Kona sun and set them in cinder and lava rock so the yard reads finished from the first week.

Irrigation

New systems, repairs and the tuning that keeps water on the roots instead of the driveway. Our owner has held the LICT irrigation certification since 2016.

Lawn & landscape maintenance

Mowing, edging, pruning and bed care on a schedule, led by an owner who has been certified in turf maintenance since 2013.

Water features & rock work

Lava-rock walls, raised planters and water gardens with lilies and taro — the kind of work you'll see in our projects below.

Certified work

Three Landscape Industry Certified Technician credentials behind every job.

The LICT test is the industry's hands-on exam, run in Kona by the Hawaii Island Landscape Association. Our owner holds it in three disciplines — each one listed on the Big Island registry.

2013

Turf maintenance

Mowing heights, feeding, weed and pest control, and the watering that keeps a Kona lawn green through the dry months.

2015

Plant installation

Setting trees, palms and shrubs so they establish — the right hole, the right depth, the right first weeks of water.

2016

Irrigation

Controllers, valves, lateral repair and head adjustment — the system that quietly decides whether everything else survives.

Our owner has also judged the tree-planting station at Kona's LICT certification test, and we're a current member of the Hawaii Island Landscape Association — a family-owned crew, not a franchise.
