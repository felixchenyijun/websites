About T&C

Designed before it's built. Built around the way you live.

Every great project starts with a plan. Before construction begins, we design your property in detailed 3D, allowing you to experience the space before it becomes a reality. Walk through your future pool, patio, outdoor kitchen, landscaping, lighting, and entertainment areas, make adjustments, and see how every element works together before we ever break ground.

With decades of hands-on experience in landscape design and construction, our team understands how to balance hardscape, planting, lighting, architecture, and functionality to create outdoor spaces that are both beautiful and built for the way you live.

Whether you're refreshing the front of your home or completely reimagining your backyard, our goal is to create a space that feels like a natural extension of your home — a private setting where you can entertain, unwind, and enjoy the outdoors for years to come.
