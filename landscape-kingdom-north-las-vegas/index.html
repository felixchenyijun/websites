Dormant tree

I got a homeowner that received not only one but several warnings letters to remove a dormant tree. HOA said tree was dead and needed to be replaced ASAP. Turf-to-desert conversions

Thinking about taking the grass out?

The Southern Nevada Water Authority's Water Smart Landscapes rebate pays single-family homes $5 per square foot for the first 10,000 square feet of grass replaced with desert landscaping — pre-inspection and terms are on snwa.com. We do the conversion end to end: grass out, drip line in, rock and desert plants in.
