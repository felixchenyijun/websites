Services

Design it, plant it, light it, keep it.

We work with residential estates — mostly country-club and waterfront homes — and larger commercial properties. One crew handles the design, the trees, the lighting, the irrigation and the weekly upkeep, so nothing falls between contractors.

I.

Landscape design & installation

Custom designs for estates and commercial property, worked out with you one-on-one. If the existing landscape has to go first, we remove it and start from a clean site.

  • Conceptual design
  • Planting design
  • Preservation design
  • Removal of existing landscape
  • Mature trees & large palms
II.

Landscape lighting

Low-voltage systems designed to be admired after dark as much as at noon — spot, flood, path and recessed fixtures placed for the plant, not the catalogue.

  • Low-voltage design
  • Transformer & fixtures
  • Spot · flood · path · recessed
  • Estate & commercial
III.

Tree services

We plant fully-grown trees and set large palms by crane, and we trim, shape and prune by species and by season. Removal and stump grinding when a tree has to come out — our tree removal experts are licensed and insured.

  • Large-palm installation
  • Trimming & shaping
  • Removal
  • Stump grinding
  • Storm-risk assessment
IV.

Irrigation

Design, installation, repairs and testing. Beachfront homes get a salt-rinse setup that washes salt off plants and surfaces with every cycle; commercial sites can run a satellite/Bluetooth-controlled system we're certified to install.

  • Design & installation
  • Repairs & testing
  • Beachfront salt rinse
  • Satellite/Bluetooth control
V.

Weekly mowing & estate maintenance

A weekly package built for your property, not a checklist — lawn, beds, sidewalks and irrigation left the way they were installed.

  • Mowing · edging · trimming
  • Blowing
  • Weeding & mulching
  • Irrigation testing
  • Annual flowers on request
