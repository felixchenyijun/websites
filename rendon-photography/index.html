About

Meet the photographer

I'm Makayla, the photographer behind Rendon Photography.

My journey with photography began long before I ever imagined it would become my career. When I was younger, my aunt introduced me to the world of photography and unknowingly planted a seed that would grow into something so much greater. Her love for the art inspired me, and as I grew older, I found myself drawn back to that same passion.

Today, I truly believe photography is part of what God has called me to do. What began as an inspiration has become a purpose — one that brings me an abundance of joy and allows me to use my creativity to serve others and preserve the moments they never want to forget.

I believe there is something incredibly beautiful about a photograph. It can hold a feeling, tell a story, and take you back to a moment long after it has passed. Whether I'm capturing the beginning of a marriage, the joy of a growing family, a milestone, a quiet moment between two people, or simply the beauty of who you are, my goal is always to create images that feel genuine, timeless, and uniquely yours.

Thank you for trusting me to capture the moments that become your forever memories.
