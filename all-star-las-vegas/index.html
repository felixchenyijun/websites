Services

What we build and maintain.

Six lines of work, one crew. Because the same people plan the turf, the pavers and the water lines, each piece is built to fit the others.

  1. Lawn care & artificial turf

    Natural lawns kept cut, edged and green — or synthetic turf laid with a clean paver border, the way most of the yards in our gallery were done.

  2. Hardscaping

    Paver patios, walkways and borders, retaining-block planter walls, circle inlays and stepping pads set into turf.

  3. Irrigation systems

    Efficient irrigation installed with the landscape, so beds and lawns get water where it counts through a Las Vegas summer.

  4. Garden design

    Desert planting that fits the house: agaves, shrubs and boulders set in rock beds, with room to grow.

  5. Tree care

    Planting, trimming and care for shade trees and palms.

  6. Residential & commercial

    Homes and commercial properties across Las Vegas — front yards, backyards and business frontage.

About

One crew, from the first sketch to the last paver.

We're a Latino-owned landscaping company based in Las Vegas, working on homes and commercial properties across the valley. Lawn care, garden design, irrigation, hardscaping and tree care all come from the same crew, so the pieces of a yard are planned together instead of patched in by three different contractors.
