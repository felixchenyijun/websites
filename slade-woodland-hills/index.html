02 — Our story

Family owned and operated since 1962.

Slade has remained a family owned and operated business since it was established in 1962 — a third-generation company, and one of the first licensed and bonded landscape contractors in the State of California. We specialize in the landscape maintenance of large commercial and industrial accounts, homeowners associations and shopping centers.

Our aim is to leave you at ease. Leave the advance planning to us: we proactively identify issues and recommend solutions, so the day-to-day of your landscape never lands on your desk.
