Our process

From the first walk-through to the finished yard.

The same four steps on every residential design, so you always know what happens next — and when.

Meet & greet

We come to the house, listen to what you want and need, and settle on a budget that suits both. We measure and photograph the areas to be redone and note every tree and feature that stays in the plan.

The plan

You get a plan of action to approve — the layout plus every material needed to build it: plants, trees, stone, mulch, soil, and lighting if you want it. We talk through changes until the design and the budget both feel right.

Approval & dates

Once the design and budget are agreed, we set a target date to begin and a date to finish. No open-ended jobs — we finish on time and do it right.

Design execution

On the agreed day our crew arrives with everything the plan calls for and transforms the space as drawn. We clean up after ourselves on every job, including maintenance visits.
